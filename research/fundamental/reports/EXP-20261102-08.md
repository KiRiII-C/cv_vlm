## Falsifiable question

EXP07 varied the source scene (TUM xyz only vs xyz+desk) but also the number of distinct training correspondences. Is any change in *blind* Bonn 5-way Recall@1 attributable to a change in training scene diversity **when training unique-correspondence count is held constant**?

This experiment tests source-support diversity, **not** a claim that two TUM trajectories constitute independent buildings or that 256 samples represent 256 unique physical locations.

## Physical data and controls

Three actual sensor recordings: TUM RGB-D Freiburg1 xyz, TUM Freiburg1 desk and Bonn RGB-D moving_obstructing_box, acquired using the original public dataset archives. Measurements are never synthesized. Original timestamps, raw RGB/depth arrays and camera GT poses determine valid 9×9 cross-view correspondences.

Two-by-two support factorial:

| Arm | Unique xyz indices | Unique desk indices | Total unique indices |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| xyz_256 | 256 | 0 | 256 |
| mixed_256 | 128 | 128 | 256 |
| xyz_512 | 512 | 0 | 512 |
| mixed_512 | 256 | 256 | 512 |

We use deterministic, nested permutations with **no replacement** before training. Within a seed, the 256 xyz portion of mixed_512 is exactly the 256 xyz portion of xyz_256; mixed_256 contains its first 128. Two seeds (0,1) and three scratch-trained models: MLP+, 4-layer RGB-D ViT, Geometry ViT (24 total runs).

Constants across all arms: fixed architecture and model initialization **within each model and seed**, 28 epochs, 15 updates per epoch, 64 distinct positive-pair indices per minibatch (without replacement), AdamW, identical hyperparameters and cosine schedule, and **the same 192 unique hard-negative xyz queries for that seed**. The hard-negative library is deliberately xyz-only, which may favor xyz-only training; document this asymmetry.

Validation checkpoint selection: TUM xyz and desk frames 64–78, with temporal separation from positive training pairs. Late TUM desk frames 82+ are supplementary evaluation; **Bonn is blind** and never used to choose a checkpoint, negative sample or hyperparameter. Bonn intrinsics and marker-to-camera GT correction follow previous protocols.

## Pre-declared estimands

For architecture \\(m\\), seed \\(s\\), support \\(n\\in\\{256,512\\}\\), define the observed blind score \\(R_{m,s,n,a}\\) as the 5-way Recall@1 for training arm \\(a\\).

Diversity contrast, matched for distinct correspondence count:

\\[
\\Delta_{m,s,n}=100\\left(R_{m,s,n,\\mathrm{mixed}}-R_{m,s,n,\\mathrm{xyz}}\\right)
\\]

Support-size contrast within each source setting:

\\[
\\Gamma_{m,s,a}=100\\left(R_{m,s,512,a}-R_{m,s,256,a}\\right)
\\]

Primary outcome: paired seed-wise blind Bonn contrast. Secondary outcome: held-out late desk score. No significance or generalization-to-buildings claim from only two seeds and one test scene. Correlated patch-level bootstrap confidence intervals are diagnostic *within sequence*, not independent building-level intervals.

## Interpretation thresholds

- If mixed outperforms xyz at matched 256 and 512 across both seeds, observe a **protocol-specific** transfer advantage; seek independent scenes and more seeds before general claims.
- If the sign changes with n, source-diversity effect is entangled with support size and requires an interaction analysis, not one headline number.
- If improvement exists only on held-out desk but not Bonn, there is no evidence for blind-domain transfer.
- If any job fails, checkpoint missing, dataset index SHA differs, negative-query SHA differs by seed, or sample counts differ, the validator **fails closed**. It must not publish a partially completed table.

Minibatches exclude exact duplicate positive indices to prevent InfoNCE false negatives caused solely by duplicate draws. Different indices can still sample overlapping physical surfaces; this is not eliminated by the sampling rule.\n\n## Limits and future science

This is point/patch-level retrieval with GT pose oracle, **not** object instance identity, scene-change detection, global relocalization, semantic mapping, or robot safety. A compelling downstream hypothesis still requires multiple physically separate annotated rooms/sessions, independently verified object IDs and change GT, and no reliance on oracle camera poses.
